# Privacy Policy

The HeadlessMc skill is a set of instructions for AI agents. It does not
collect, store or send any personal data, and it has no servers of its own.

When you use the skill, your agent may run HeadlessMc or other tools on your
computer. These can contact third parties such as Microsoft (account login),
Mojang, mod loader sites, Modrinth or GitHub. Their privacy policies apply to
that data. Your AI agent's provider handles your conversations under its own
privacy policy.

Questions: https://github.com/headlesshq/headlessmc-skill/issues
